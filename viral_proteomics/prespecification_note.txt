PRE-SPECIFICATION NOTE (recorded on 13 October 2026)

Caveat: this note was written after the analysis had been run. It reconstructs the design
agreed before the overlap was examined.

Bait: nsp13 only. No backup bait.
Background: 19,825 genes in the DESeq2 object (at least 10 counts in at least 19 of 71 samples).
Ranking: DESeq2 Wald statistic.
Primary contrast: UC vs non-IBD. Secondary: CD vs non-IBD.
Primary test: fgsea across baits; rank baits by absolute NES, two-sided, direction reported separately.
Null control: 1,000 random gene sets matched on mean expression (10 bins).
Positive control: REACTOME_INTERFERON_SIGNALING and REACTOME_ISG15_ANTIVIRAL_MECHANISM, Holm across the two.
Minimum size: fewer than 5 expressed interactors = underpowered.
